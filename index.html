Security & Usage Notice

Secure Vault Pro runs locally in your browser. Before you continue, please confirm the basics below.

  • Zero-storage workflow: Closing or refreshing the tab clears your local session and any decrypted content.
  • Legitimate use only: Use the vault, audit, and chat features for personal, legal, or business privacy work only.
  • Browser limits still apply: The app can protect transport and local handling, but it cannot block screenshots or a peer recording their own screen.
