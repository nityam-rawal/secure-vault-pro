# Secure Vault Pro India Launch Playbook

## Positioning

Secure Vault Pro should be framed as a cyber safety assistant, not just a privacy tool.

- Lead with scam survival, not encryption jargon.
- Talk about WhatsApp fraud, fake KYC, digital arrest, UPI collect scams, and recovery steps.
- Use Hindi, Hinglish, and local examples before technical language.

## Main Promise

"Scam dekho, risk samjho, aur turant action lo."

## Content Pillars

1. Scam Decoder demos
2. Fast recovery checklists
3. Family safety alerts
4. Real-world India scam breakdowns
5. School, office, and RWA awareness kits

## Weekly Content Mix

1. Two Instagram Reels using the in-app Reel Hook copy
2. Three WhatsApp-forward cards using the Family Alert template
3. One LinkedIn or X post with a simple scam teardown
4. One local workshop or webinar clip
5. One "what to do in the first 10 minutes" post

## Distribution Channels

- WhatsApp family groups
- Instagram Reels
- YouTube Shorts
- Telegram communities
- RWAs and housing groups
- Colleges and coaching centers
- Small businesses and branch offices

## Sample Hooks

- "Digital arrest real nahi hota, panic real hota hai."
- "UPI mein paise lene ke liye PIN nahi dalte."
- "KYC ke naam par app install? Red flag."
- "Refund message ka matlab aksar refund nahi, fraud hota hai."
- "Jo scam aaj aap par fail hua, kal family par ho sakta hai."

## Local Partnerships

- Cyber awareness volunteers
- Bank branch staff
- School principals and college societies
- Resident welfare associations
- MSME associations
- Community legal and cyber safety groups

## Conversion Path

1. Short video or WhatsApp card
2. Open the app
3. Paste suspicious message in Scam Decoder
4. Run the audit
5. Copy the family alert and spread it
